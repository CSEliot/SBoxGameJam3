# Bottoms Up: One More Round

A multiplayer pub-crawl runner for s&box. Think **Pepsiman meets Crazy Taxi**: pure carnage, chaos, and liquor. Aim to be the best drunkard — climb to the top, or risk it all.

## How to Win

- You're always running **forward** — you can't stop, you can't die. Just steer.
- Follow the **arrow above your head** to the next pub, and **chug, chug, CHUG!**
- Every second you're on the street you earn points based on how **drunk** you are.
- The more you drink, the harder the game: you move **faster** and it's easier to **fall over**.
- The more you drink, the more **time** gets added to reach the **next bar**.
- The clock starts at **00:30** when you leave the first bar. **If it hits zero, you lose ALL YOUR POINTS.** So at every pub, decide: cash in, or risk it all for... **ANOTHER ROUND!!**

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **A / D** | Tilt left and right. Think of it like you're driving a unicycle — one tap both turns you and corrects your lean. Lean too far and you eat pavement. |
| **Shift (hold)** | Keep your pants **up**! You silly sloppy drunk, KEEP YOUR PANTS UP! |
| **Shift (release)** | Drop them for a quick moment of **accurate** turns — at the cost of speed and a pathetic jump. |
| **Spacebar** | You can **jump!** At the bar it's the chug key: **SMASH SPACEBAR!** before the round timer runs out. |
| **R** | Rescue — zeroes your speed and teleports you back to base if you're stuck. (ABUSE THIS == BAN!)|
| **Esc** | Pause menu. |

## At the Bar

Entering a bar mid-run pauses everything and asks **"ONE MORE ROUND?"** (the very first bar skips the small talk — you chug straight away):

- **CASH IN SCORE** — end your run and bank your points.
- **PLAY MINI-GAME** — *drink more, get drunker, get time.* Mash spacebar to fill the chug meter before the round ends; every press counts toward the next beer, 
   and whole beers convert into extra seconds on the clock. Time won is added when you burst back out the door.

Trips matter: up to a point, the drunker you are, the further back you get rewound when you get up.

## Multiplayer & Leaderboards

Pub-crawl the city alongside other players and compete for the **BEST DRUNKARDS** leaderboard — your best single run is submitted to the s&box backend automatically. 
The end screen tallies your score, beers drunk, and bars visited (with a well-earned *WASTED* stamp if you timed out at zero).

## The Look

A dusk that quickly becomes night: glowing windows, street lamps, cartoonish Pixar-esque mid-low-poly streets, and Citizen runners in whatever fancy dress their account carries, all under chunky neon type.
